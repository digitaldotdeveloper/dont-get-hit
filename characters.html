Cosmetic slots

One animated chicken, many interchangeable layers. Nothing below duplicates an animation — each item hangs off a pivot and inherits the motion.

  • head — backwards cap, bucket hat
  • face — shades
  • neck — gold chain, aviator scarf
  • body — hoodie
  • back — free for capes and packs (no jetpack: he has wings)

Try loadouts live by adding ?wear=head:hat_bucket,neck:scarf_art to the game URL, or ?wear=head:none for the bare bird.
