Privacy Policy

DON'T GET HIT · last updated 10 September 2026

This policy explains what the game does with data. It is written to be read, not to be survived — if something here is unclear, the contact address at the bottom is a real one.

The short version: the game does not ask for your name, your email or your account. It keeps your progress on your own device. It collects anonymous gameplay statistics so we can tell whether the game is any good, and it shows ads you choose to watch.

What is stored on your device

Your progress lives in your device's local browser storage and never leaves it unless you ask it to. That is:

  • Your best score and distance
  • Your eggs, upgrades, vehicles and unlocked looks
  • Your mission board and daily-reward streak
  • Your settings (sound, vibration, reduced motion)

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage, deletes all of it permanently. There is no cloud backup, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

What is collected

The game records anonymous gameplay events so we can see what is working: when a session starts and ends, how long a run lasted, how far it got, what ended it, which missions were completed, which upgrades were bought, and whether a rewarded video was watched or declined.

Each event carries a randomly generated session identifier and the day-count since first launch. It does not carry your name, your email address, your phone number, your contacts, your location, your device identifier, or anything typed by you. The identifier is regenerated and is not linked to you across apps.

Crash reports

If the game hits an error, it records the error message, the file and line it came from, and what the game was doing at the time. This is used only to fix the bug.

Advertising

The game shows rewarded video ads — ones you choose to watch in exchange for something, such as reviving a run or doubling the eggs you just earned. Ad providers may use an advertising identifier to decide which ad to show and to count views. Their handling of that identifier is governed by their own privacy policies, and your device settings let you reset or limit that identifier at any time.

Buying No Forced Ads removes ads that interrupt you. Rewarded videos stay available, because they are how the revive and the daily reward are paid for.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by the app store, not by us. We receive confirmation that a purchase happened. We never see your card details or your billing address.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and it will be removed.

Your choices

  • Turn off analytics and crash reports — clearing the app's storage removes any queued events, and uninstalling stops collection entirely.
  • Reset your advertising identifier — in your device's privacy settings.
  • Delete everything — uninstall the game, or clear its storage. Because nothing is stored on a server, that is a complete deletion.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, the date at the top changes and the new version appears here before the change ships.

Contact
